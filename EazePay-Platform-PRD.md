## 1. What EazePay Is

EazePay is the **agentic financial infrastructure behind care and service brands**. It is the orchestration layer — not the lender, not the card processor. It captures a customer at the point of sale (or from paid/social channels), runs a soft-pull on their real financials, builds a complete, consented application, and routes only **pre-qualified** files into a ranked lender marketplace — then settles funds **merchant-direct**.

A lender that plugs into EazePay gets two things they actually want: **volume** (generated by our verticals + distribution) and **quality** (every file is soft-pulled and pre-qualified before it reaches them).

The platform is exposed to the market as a family of **vertical brands** — each a white-labeled wrapper over the same rail.

---

## 2. The Problem

Service and care businesses (medical, trades, coaching, veterinary) lose high-ticket sales ($2k–$50k) because the customer can't pay cash and:

- Card is the only option at the counter — large tickets drop off.
- Consumer BNPL is small-dollar and not merchant-controlled.
- Integrating multiple lenders is N separate tech builds, each with its own contract and webhook.
- Leads from Meta / Google / Instagram arrive raw — no financial signal, no routing, manual chase.
- Lenders, in turn, get raw leads, not qualified borrowers — low pull-through, wasted underwriting.

EazePay solves this by owning the capture → qualification → routing layer, so merchants get instant financing at the point of sale and lenders get pre-qualified borrowers.

---

## 3. The Rail — How It Works

```
Pixie  ── lead capture + smart routing (Meta / Google / Instagram + point of sale)
  ↓
HighSale ── soft-pull enrichment & qualification (credit · income · available credit · DTI; zero score impact)
  ↓
Smart routing ── high-ticket / low-ticket funnel, decided on the real financial data
  ↓
Sales call ── human close where it lifts conversion
  ↓
Finance application ── complete, consented file
  ↓
Decision engine + Lender marketplace ── ranked, parallel; FCRA/Reg B reason codes
  ↓
Merchant-direct payout ── the winning lender funds the business directly
```

**Key principles**

- **EazePay is the orchestration layer.** We are not the lender and never custody consumer funds. Lenders make the credit decision and fund the merchant.
- **Pixie** is the intelligent lead-capture form — smart routing is built into the form; it ingests leads from Meta/Google/Instagram and routes each to a high- or low-ticket funnel on the financial data HighSale returns.
- **HighSale** is the soft-pull / financial-enrichment engine and its agents.
- **Card processing (MiCamp)** is a **separate rail** from the loan path. EazePay is a registered ISO under MiCamp for everyday card payments; it is not in the consumer-loan flow.
- **The decision engine + lender marketplace** is our own layer (ranked, parallel routing, reason codes) — the differentiator vs. pure aggregators.

## 6. Products & Surfaces

### 6.1 Pixie — the consumer application

The branded lead-capture + application surface (embeddable widget + hosted apply flow at `checkout.eazepay.com` / `apply.<brand>`). Captures the customer, takes soft-pull consent inline (FCRA + e-sign), collects intake, and hands a structured, consented applicant to HighSale. Mobile-first; card data tokenised via TransArmor (SAQ-A).

### 6.2 Partner Portal (`merchant.eazepay.com`)

Each partner (practice/clinic/contractor) gets a portal scoped to their own book: dashboard (submissions, approvals, funded volume, pending payout, FICO mix), submit-application + send-link (text/email/copy/QR), live application tracking, **insights/reporting** (approval rate, funded loans & volume, take-up, pull-through, decision-to-fund, avg loan size), settlements, team & roles, API keys. Real auth via Clerk.

### 6.3 Admin "Command Centre" (`admin.eazepay.com`)

Internal ops: every application across every partner and vertical; full **partner management** (drill into a partner → profile, users & roles, lender access, suspend/approve); **lender network** configuration; approvals queue; audit log; queues / dead-letter; reports.

### 6.4 Decision Engine + Lender Marketplace

Our own waterfall/propensity layer: takes the soft-pulled file, applies eligibility + tiering, fires across the eligible lender panel in parallel, returns offers ranked by total cost of credit, and produces FCRA/Reg B-compliant reason codes on decline. (This is the layer that differentiates us from feeding volume into a third-party aggregator.)

## 8. Launch Scope & Plan — Path to First Real Loan

**The goal (end of July, on staging):** a **real consumer completes a loan end-to-end** — apply → soft-pull consent → intake → real offers back → **all offers shown** → render the loan doc → click to confirm → **lender funds the merchant**. **No mocks on the lender path.** Plus: a merchant logs in with **real auth** and onboards.

### 8.1 Two tracks (sequenced — do not whipsaw the team)

**Track A — Revenue bridge (now).** Fund real loans through the **already-active aggregator (Magwitch)** on the skinny consumer flow. This is the fastest path to revenue and de-risks the possibility that US Bank / CareCredit reject us or slip. It also delivers the multi-offer "all offers shown" experience a single direct lender won't. _Do not add a net-new Skeps integration to the critical path_ — Skeps is a competitor we're building the decision engine to beat, and a fresh integration adds the very delay the bridge exists to remove. Only revisit if Magwitch has a concrete, named blocker (can't fund in prod / approval rates / economics / ToS).

**Track B — The moat (parallel).** **US Bank "Flex"** direct adapter + **start US Bank's third-party security / vendor review immediately** (this is the real long pole — 8–12 weeks, outside our control) + the **product-type abstraction** (see 8.4). **Covered Care** and **CareCredit** are added on the same adapter pattern as they clear.

### 8.2 Scope discipline (hold the line)

- **1–2 lenders only.** No marketplace UI, no fancy routing yet.
- **Fill the scaffolds / wire real providers into the ports.** Avoid re-architecting schema, orchestration, reason codes, webhook contract, or brand namespacing.
- **Everything feature-flagged**, default off.
- **Parked adapters:** ChargeAfter, CoverCare, EngineTech, Queen Street, Skeps.

### 8.3 Deliberately deferred

- **KYB (Middesk)** and **bank verification (Plaid)** — US Bank handles merchant underwriting & funding for the direct path.
- **DocuSign (the vendor)** — the consumer loan is render-PDF-then-confirm. **But e-sign compliance is NOT deferred** (see 12).
- **MiCamp in the loan path** — card processing is a separate rail; not needed to fund a loan.

### 8.4 The one sanctioned schema change — product type

The offer object is installment-only today (`{ term, apr, monthly, tag? }`). **CareCredit is revolving / deferred-interest**, which the installment shape cannot represent honestly (and shoehorning it breaks TILA/Reg Z disclosure + adverse-action correctness). Add an **additive, backward-compatible, feature-flagged** `productType` discriminator on the offer:

- `installment` — `apr`, `termMonths`, `monthlyPayment`, `amount`, `financeCharge`
- `revolving_deferred_interest` — `creditLimit`, `promoApr` (e.g. 0%), `promoMonths`, `postPromoApr`, `minMonthlyPayment`, `deferredInterest: true`
- `bnpl` — short-term installments / pay-in-N

Default `installment`. Implement US Bank (installment) end-to-end first; light up the revolving shape when CareCredit is wired. This is foundational — every future BNPL / lease-to-own lender reuses it.

### 8.5 Non-deferrable before any real loan

- **Fail-closed lender webhook signature verification** (currently fail-open — see 11).
- **ESIGN/UETA-valid** loan confirmation.
- **Written data-handling / access-control policies** for everyone touching consumer PII (US Bank attestation).

### 8.6 Hosting

Stay on **Railway** through launch and first real loans. AWS Terraform is written and ready; **AWS is an explicit post-launch decision** (US Bank's security review may ask about hosting controls — be ready to speak to Railway's posture, but don't migrate now).

### 8.7 Success criteria (definition of done for the sprint)

1. One real consumer funds a loan end-to-end on staging through a real lender path (bridge or US Bank), no mocks.
2. A merchant signs in with real auth and completes onboarding.
3. Lender webhooks fail-closed; e-sign is compliant; PII-touch policies signed.
4. Investor dashboard shows real revenue + ad spend (see 16).

## 9. Lenders & Decision Engine

### 9.1 Lender model (current)

Lenders are **database-driven config** consumed by a `LendersService` registry; inbound events are routed by a `lender:<lenderId>` source header. `Lender` fields include: `name`, `type` (`DTC | MERCHANT | BNPL`), `isActive`, `eazepayReferralFeePercent`, `eazepayPerDealFee`, `applicationUrlTemplate`, `allowedIndustries[]`, `allowedStates[]`, `minCreditScore`/`maxCreditScore`, `minAmount`/`maxAmount`, `webhookUrl`, `webhookSecret` (encrypted), `metadata`.

### 9.2 Adapter "port" (what an integration implements)

Today this is a registry, not a formal `ILenderAdapter` interface. Building US Bank Flex = a **real adapter**, not just filling a stub: quote/offer, decision, webhook normalisation, and funding. Recommended near-term: define a thin `LenderAdapter` port (`quote()`, `submit()`, `normalizeWebhook()`) so US Bank / CareCredit / aggregator each implement the same contract behind feature flags.

### 9.3 Offer / quote shape

Installment-only today (`term`, `apr`, `monthly`, `tag?`). Evolve via the `productType` discriminator in 8.4.

### 9.4 Reason codes (mature — do not re-architect)

`Decision` stores `tier`, `eligible`, `policyVersion`, `reasonCodes[]`. `reason-code.service.ts` maps bureau factor codes (VantageScore/FICO) and lender decline codes to consumer-facing strings, de-dupes, and **caps at 4 per Reg B §1002.9(b)(2)** for adverse action. FCRA §615 covered.

### 9.5 Webhook contract

Generic inbound mapping normalises lender status → `APPROVED | DECLINED | FUNDED` (with `approvedAmount`, `apr`, `term`, `lenderApplicationId`). **Gap:** signature verification is currently permissive on the lender path and must go fail-closed per lender before go-live.

### 9.6 Lender roadmap

- **Bridge:** Magwitch (active aggregator) — breadth + revenue now.
- **Direct:** US Bank (Flex) → Covered Care → CareCredit (needs `productType`).
- **Parked:** ChargeAfter, CoverCare, EngineTech, Queen Street, Skeps.
- _Note:_ the named direct adapters are not on `main` — they're on the held decision-engine branch; confirm the build base.

## 12. Compliance & Legal

| Requirement             | Position                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **US Bank attestation** | **Long pole.** Complete their third-party/vendor security review; bring everyone touching consumer data (Amala + Eaze) under written data-handling + access-control policies; produce a data-flow / who-touches-PII map. Start now, parallel to code. |
| **SOC 2**               | Not waiting on Type 2 (6–12mo observation). Use the attestation/questionnaire path with US Bank.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **FCRA**                | Soft-pull permissible purpose; §615 adverse-action reasons in the rail. HighSale/lenders hold the bureau relationships.                                                                                                                               |
| **ECOA / Reg B**        | Reason codes capped at 4, machine-readable, adverse-action within 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **TILA / Reg Z**        | Offer disclosures must be product-accurate — installment vs **deferred-interest** (CareCredit) need different disclosures; do not misrepresent (8.4).                                                                                                 |
| **ESIGN / UETA**        | Click-to-confirm loan agreement must be valid: e-consent, retainable/printable record, clear attribution. (Defer the DocuSign _vendor_, not e-sign.)                                                                                                  |
| **PCI DSS**             | SAQ-A — card data tokenised via TransArmor, never on EazePay servers.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **PII**                 | Classify before write, encrypt SSN/EIN/bank, tenant-scope, audit access, retention. Field-level encryption is an open gap.                                                                                                                            |

> Honest note: the attestation, the written policies, the e-sign legal sufficiency, and an eventual external pen test / SOC 2 are **human/expert work** — not something the code alone closes.

## 13. Security Model

- **Card data:** never touches EazePay (TransArmor iframe → token → MiCamp). SAQ-A.
- **API keys:** bcrypt-hashed, shown once, masked thereafter; `pk_live_` / `sk_live_` prefixes.
- **Auth:** Clerk JWT (RS256, JWKS cached) for portals; hashed API-key guard for merchant API; roles `OWNER/ADMIN/VIEWER/SUPERADMIN/OPS/FINANCE/SUPPORT`.
- **Multi-tenancy:** every merchant-scoped query filters by `organizationId`; tenant isolation at the service layer.
- **Webhooks:** must verify signatures fail-closed (current gap on lender path).
- **PII encryption:** 256-bit field-level for SSN/EIN/bank — required before scale (KMS envelope or pgcrypto).
- **CORS:** API whitelist; `/widget.js` ACAO `*`; `/widget` iframe `frame-ancestors *`; everything else `X-Frame-Options: DENY`.

## 16. Investor / Sales-Intelligence Dashboard

**Need (near-term, ~2 weeks):** David wants a dashboard for a prospective investor. Minimum: **revenue + ad spend**. Build the endpoints so the fuller set is available: close/win-loss rate, # sales, revenue from sales, # leads, ad spend → CAC.

**Approach:** reuse the existing `financing-funnel` + `revenue` reporting queries; add a **platform-wide (admin) rollup** (current reporting is merchant-scoped), plus:

- **Leads / top-of-funnel** from **HighSale**.
- **Ad spend** from Meta / Google (start with manual/CSV if needed; API later) → **CAC**.
- **Sales / win-loss** from the loan outcomes (`Decision` + funded events) and CRM.

**Guardrails:** keep this **off the lender critical path** (separable read-only reporting layer, same pattern as a standalone intelligence hub). Every number must be **defensible from source of truth** — it's investor-facing; define each metric precisely, no vanity inflation.
